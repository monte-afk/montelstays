How it works

Simple from your end.
Handled from ours.

You tell us what the family needs. We take it from there.

Step one
Tell us what you need
Submit a brief in under two minutes. Suburb, size, duration, any special requirements. No claim numbers, no sensitive data required.
Step two
We find the match
We contact our vetted host network and present you with the best options — same business day. Right suburb, right size, furnished and ready.
Step three
Focus on what matters
We handle the coordination, documentation and extensions. You focus on the claim, the move, the new job, the kids' school — whatever comes next.

For property owners

Your property.
A better kind of booking.

Sydney hosts are navigating 180-night STR caps, demanding short-stay guests, and rising platform fees. Displacement bookings are different — and better.

Longer stays, less turnover Placements typically run 3 weeks to 3 months. Fewer changeovers, fewer cleans, less admin.
Respectful guests Families between homes treat your property like a home — because it is. Not a holiday, not a party.
STR cap friendly Displacement stays may not count toward your 180-night annual limit. More income from your property, year-round.
Lower fees than major platforms We charge less than Airbnb. And only on confirmed bookings — never upfront.
We handle everything Brief coordination, documentation, extensions. You accept or decline — it's always your call.
